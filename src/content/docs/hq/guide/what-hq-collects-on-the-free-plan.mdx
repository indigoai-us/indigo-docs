Using HQ for free includes sharing usage data with us. It helps us see what
people use and fix what breaks. Adding a card to your company turns this into
a setting you can switch off.

<Aside type="note">
Effective October 22, 2026 (disclosure version 2026-10-22). The same terms are
in the [Privacy Policy](https://www.hqforwork.com/privacy) under "Usage data on
the free plan" and in section 5 of the
[Terms of Service](https://www.hqforwork.com/terms).
</Aside>

## What changed

Before October 22, 2026, usage data was a setting you could turn off on any
plan. From October 22, 2026, sharing usage data is part of using HQ for free,
when your company has no card on file. If your company has a card on file,
usage data stays a setting you can turn off.

- **New on the free plan:** HQ shows this disclosure during setup. Collection
  starts after you click Continue.
- **Already using HQ for free:** the HQ desktop app shows a notice with the
  date collection starts, three days after you first see it.

## What is collected

- Usage counts
- Skill and command names
- Timings
- Model names
- Company and person ids
- App version and operating system

## What is never collected

- File contents
- Secrets
- Prompts and outputs
- Vault data

## Two ways to turn it off

1. **Add a card to your company.** Collection becomes a setting you can turn
   off. See [Upgrade](#upgrade) below.
2. **Run HQ self-hosted.** HQ core and the HQ CLI run on your own machines
   with no HQ account and send no usage data. See
   [Use HQ free, self-hosted or on-prem](/hq/guide/use-hq-for-free/).

## Upgrade

1. Sign in at [hq.computer](https://hq.computer).
2. Open your company's **Billing**.
3. Add a card.

Usage data then becomes a setting you can turn off. For how billing works,
see [Billing and usage](/hq/products/hq-pro/billing-and-usage/).
